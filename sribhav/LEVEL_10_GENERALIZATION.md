# Level 10 — Generalization

> **Level of progression:** after this level you know whether the instability predictor and
> adaptive-routing mechanism generalize beyond the model and workload they were built on.
> This is the capstone of the optional ladder.
>
> **Optional milestone:** not required for the core milestone. Everything from Level 5
> onward is optional.

Source phase: *Phase 10* of [`../SRIBHAV_PLAN_REVISED_FULL.md`](../SRIBHAV_PLAN_REVISED_FULL.md)

After the mechanism works, test:

* additional VQA datasets
* different LLaVA variants
* different VisPruner configurations
* potentially another visual-token reduction technique

The question is no longer:

> Does VisPruner work on more datasets?

The stronger question is:

> **Does the instability predictor and adaptive-routing mechanism generalize beyond the
> model/workload on which it was developed?**

That is a legitimate generalization experiment.
